# EXPLORE file template

Copy this into `EXPLORE-<idea-slug>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` at Stage 1 and fill it as each stage runs.

```markdown
# Explore: <idea name>

Started: <date>. Mode: venture | builder. Decision owner: <role>.

## 1. Intake
Idea: <two sentences>
For: <a specific person, role, consequence>
Problem (their words): <quote or close paraphrase, with source>
Why now: <what changed>
Kill condition (written <date>, before research): <finding that makes this a bad idea>

### Forcing questions
| Question | Answer | Evidence type (behaviour / money / opinion / none) |
|---|---|---|

### Premises
| Premise | Status (verified / believed / unknown) | How we would know |
|---|---|---|

### Alternatives to building
- Do nothing: keep the current workaround. Cost: <...>
- <alternative 2>

## 2. Research
### Market and competitors
| Product | What it does | Price | Strength | Weakness | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Graveyard: <who tried, why they failed, source>
### Substitutes
### Users and jobs
### Feasibility
### Moat and data
### Unit economics (if run)

## 3. Personas and journeys

## 4. Assumptions
| Assumption | Risk type | Importance (H/M/L) | Evidence (H/M/L) | Cheapest test |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Riskiest assumption test
Assumption: <...>
Test: <what we run>
Kill bar (set <date>): <result that kills>
Fund bar (set <date>): <result that funds the next step>
Deadline: <date>
Disposal plan: <what happens to the probe afterwards>

## 5. Positioning

## 6. PRD
<eight parts, with citations>
### Not doing (and why)

## 7. Verdict
```

- [ ] Target outcome and metric named
- [ ] Guardrail metrics named
